UAW Local 647 has represented employees across greater Cincinnati for more than eight decades — bargaining fair contracts, defending members through the grievance process, and building power on the shop floor. Our Executive Board, Bargaining Committee, and unit-level stewards work every day to make sure every member's voice is heard.
