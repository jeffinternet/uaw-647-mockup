Grievance Form

If you believe your rights under the collective bargaining agreement have been violated, you have the right to file a grievance. Use the steps below to get started, or contact your steward directly.

Step 1: Talk to Your Steward

Before filing formally, discuss the issue with your unit steward. Many issues can be resolved informally at this stage.

Step 2: Submit a Written Grievance

If the issue isn't resolved, complete the grievance form below with as much detail as possible, including dates, witnesses, and the specific contract article you believe was violated.
