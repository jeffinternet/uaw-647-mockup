About the Local

Our History

1942 — Chartered

UAW Local 647 was chartered, representing workers in the greater Cincinnati area from the outset.

Decades of Bargaining

Over the following decades, the Local negotiated successive contracts, building wages, benefits, and workplace protections for its members.

Today

UAW Local 647 continues to represent members across greater Cincinnati, guided by the same principles of solidarity the Local was founded on.
